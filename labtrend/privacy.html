Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Who is responsible

Labtrend is developed by Wojciech Parys (Poland), who is the data controller for the limited data described below. Contact: wojciech.parys93@gmail.com.

Your results stay on your phone

Labtrend has no external database and no user accounts. Your lab results, notes and settings are stored on your device, and in your phone's own backup (for example Google backup) if you have it turned on; we have no server and never receive them. The developer does not receive, store or process your health data. Labtrend has no analytics or crash-reporting service.

Reading a PDF with AI (optional)

If you choose to read a PDF with AI, Labtrend sends it directly from your device to the AI provider you picked in Settings (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI or OpenRouter), using your own API key, which is kept in your device's secure storage. Nothing passes through any server of ours and we never receive your PDF or its content.

What is sent depends on the provider. Anthropic and OpenAI receive the PDF file in the request (OpenAI is asked not to store the response). xAI receives the file as a temporary upload, which Labtrend deletes right after reading and which expires after one hour in any case. With OpenRouter (the free option), the PDF never leaves your phone: its text is first recognised on your device with the phone's own text recognition, and only that text is sent; OpenRouter passes it to the model provider it selects, and free models may have their own data terms. If a report is too long to read in one go, Labtrend sends the text recognised on your device in several parts to the same provider.

The provider processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy, and you have your own relationship with it through your key.

If you choose to organise your tests with AI (to combine tests saved under different names), Labtrend sends the same provider only the names of your tests, their units, categories and how many results each has. No values, dates, labs or PDFs are sent, and nothing changes until you confirm the suggested changes.

Typing results in by hand, or copying a prompt to use with your own chatbot, sends nothing from Labtrend. What you paste into another service is between you and that service.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. All ad requests are non-personalised. Labtrend sends the ad SDK no keywords, no content and none of your results, and banners appear only on the Settings and Support screens, never on screens that show results, tests or charts (including Home). In addition, when you open the app (not on the first launch, and at most once every four hours) a skippable full-screen Google AdMob app open ad may cover the start of the app before you see your data; it is dropped if it does not load within a few seconds.

Google AdMob may still collect device and usage information, such as the advertising ID, IP address and diagnostics, as described in Google's policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). In the EEA and UK we ask for your consent with Google's consent form (UMP) before any ad is requested. Where consent is required, you can reopen it in Settings under "Privacy & ads settings". Subscribers who remove ads see no ads.

Purchases

Labtrend Pro (monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) and one-time tips are sold through Google Play, which handles payment; we never see your card details. Purchases are verified by RevenueCat, which acts as our processor. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase and receipt information and basic device and platform information. It never receives your results.

Legal bases: performance of the contract (providing what you bought), consent (ad consent where required) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud.

Data on your device

Language, app lock and similar preferences are stored on your device. The optional app lock uses your device's biometrics or passcode through the operating system; biometric data never reaches Labtrend. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything stored on the device; a copy in your phone's own backup is managed through your Google or Apple account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data we hold, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. As we hold almost no personal data, most requests concern Google (ads, purchases) or RevenueCat; we will help you reach them. You may complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO) or to your local authority.

Children

Labtrend is not directed at children under 16.

Not a medical device

Labtrend shows the results you enter, their reference ranges and trends. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice and is not a medical device. Talk to your doctor about your results.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions or requests: wojciech.parys93@gmail.com.

Annunci

La versione gratuita mostra annunci di Google AdMob. Tutte le richieste di annunci sono non personalizzate. Labtrend non passa all'SDK pubblicitario parole chiave, contenuti né i tuoi risultati, e i banner compaiono solo nelle schermate Impostazioni e Sostegno, mai in quelle con risultati, esami o grafici (nemmeno in Home). Inoltre, all'apertura dell'app (non al primo avvio e al massimo una volta ogni quattro ore) può comparire un annuncio a schermo intero di apertura app di Google AdMob, che si può saltare; se non si carica entro pochi secondi, non viene mostrato.

Google AdMob può comunque raccogliere informazioni sul dispositivo e sull'uso, come l'ID pubblicitario, l'indirizzo IP e dati diagnostici, come descritto nelle norme di Google (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Nel SEE e nel Regno Unito chiediamo il tuo consenso con il modulo di Google (UMP) prima di richiedere qualsiasi annuncio. Dove il consenso è richiesto, puoi riaprirlo in Impostazioni, sotto «Privacy e impostazioni degli annunci». Chi si abbona e rimuove gli annunci non li vede.

Acquisti

Labtrend Pro (abbonamento mensile o annuale, o acquisto unico per sempre) e le mance una tantum sono venduti tramite Google Play, che gestisce il pagamento; non vediamo mai i dati della tua carta. Gli acquisti sono verificati da RevenueCat, che agisce come nostro responsabile del trattamento. RevenueCat riceve un ID utente anonimo dell'app, informazioni su acquisto e ricevuta e informazioni di base su dispositivo e piattaforma, mai i tuoi risultati.

Basi giuridiche: esecuzione del contratto (fornire quanto acquistato), consenso (per gli annunci, dove richiesto) e legittimo interesse a prevenire le frodi.

Dati sul tuo dispositivo

Lingua, blocco dell'app e preferenze simili sono salvati sul tuo dispositivo. Il blocco facoltativo usa la biometria o il codice del dispositivo tramite il sistema operativo; i dati biometrici non raggiungono mai Labtrend. Disinstallare l'app o cancellarne i dati elimina tutto ciò che è salvato sul dispositivo; una copia nel backup del telefono si gestisce dal tuo account Google o Apple.

I tuoi diritti

Ai sensi del GDPR puoi chiedere accesso, rettifica, cancellazione, limitazione o portabilità dei dati personali che deteniamo, opporti al trattamento e revocare il consenso in qualsiasi momento. Poiché deteniamo pochissimi dati personali, la maggior parte delle richieste riguarda Google (annunci, acquisti) o RevenueCat; ti aiuteremo a contattarli. Puoi presentare reclamo all'autorità polacca per la protezione dei dati (UODO) o a quella del tuo paese.

Minori

Labtrend non è rivolta ai minori di 16 anni.

Non è un dispositivo medico

Labtrend mostra i risultati che inserisci, i relativi intervalli di riferimento e gli andamenti. Non fa diagnosi, non cura, non dà consigli medici e non è un dispositivo medico. Parla con il tuo medico dei tuoi risultati.
